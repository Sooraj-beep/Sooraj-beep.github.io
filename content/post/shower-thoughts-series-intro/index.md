Looks like the title was catchy enough for you to click on it so welcome! This is something that I've wanted to work on for the longest time but never got around to it.
Today I finally got to it so here we are. The Shower Thoughts Series is meant to be my creative outlet to talk about anything and everything that comes to my mind (the most intriguing of these topics happen to incept during showers, thus the title).

Here are some ground rules for how this series will work:

- **No AI generated content:** Yep, thats right. Everything I upload under this section will be my own words coming straight from my brain. Nothing will be passed through an LLM to polish or generate content. Why? because I believe the content I generate in this manner is truly my own. It will include errors and verbiage you may not see elsewhere but that's the point. It looks off because it is uniquely mine.

- **No Googling during the writeup of content:** I will not be googling anything during the writeup of each topic in this series. I know this has some downsides: I may not be factually checking statements, statistics I mention may be off, there may be spelling errors. Let me make it clear that nothing in this series should be used to cite any work simply because nothing in this series has been cited and thoroughly researched. Everything thats shared here is straight from my brain. That being said, if you really want to use my work for something, don't ask, just go for it.

- **Everything is fair game:** I will write about anything and everything in this series. They will span across multiple themes and topics and the only metric I will use on what to write about will be based on how much that topic interests me.

With the rules laid out, we are all good to go. Expect content of varying quality released in an unknown frequency for the foreseeable future :).
